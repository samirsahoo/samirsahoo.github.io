Strategic AI architecture handbook

Enterprise AI & Agentic AI Knowledge Hub

Understand the concepts. Draw the architecture. Ace the interview.

Strategic AI architecture explained simply: LLMs and model selection, the enterprise AI stack, RAG and GraphRAG, vector databases, evaluation, agents, MCP and multi-agent patterns, Spring AI, AI security and governance — with diagrams, system designs and interview questions.

Choose a learning pathInterview preparation

This handbook explains enterprise AI the way an architect needs it: what each building block does, why it exists, how the pieces fit into a system, and what can go wrong. Every article follows the same structure: a simple definition, the problem it solves, diagrams of the architecture and the request flow, trade-offs, common mistakes, and interview questions with answers.

The handbook has four modules and an interview-preparation section:

  1. LLM Foundations & the Enterprise AI Stack: how large language models work, inference and cost, prompting, fine-tuning, model selection, the reference stack and LLMOps.
  2. RAG, Vector Databases & GraphRAG: retrieval-augmented generation from first principles to hybrid search, reranking, GraphRAG and evaluation, plus a full system design.
  3. Agentic AI, MCP & Multi-Agent Systems: the agent loop, tool calling, the Model Context Protocol, workflow patterns, multi-agent topologies, memory and reliability.
  4. Spring AI, AI Security & Governance: building all of the above in Java with Spring AI 2.0, then securing and governing it.
  5. Interview Preparation: a framework for AI system design interviews, a question bank and a one-page cheat sheet.
