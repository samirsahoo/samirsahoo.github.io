Data, distributed systems & security handbook

Data, Distributed Systems & Cybersecurity Knowledge Hub

Understand the data. Trust the system. Check yourself.

Data and security architecture explained simply: Kafka, event-driven systems and stream processing; distributed transactions, CQRS, Saga and consistency; data platforms, governance and data security; threat modelling, AI security and FinTech regulations, with interactive diagrams, system designs and quick checks.

Choose a learning pathSelf review & quick check

This handbook covers the parts of enterprise architecture where data moves between systems, systems disagree, and attackers and regulators pay attention. For each building block it explains what it does, why it exists, how the pieces fit together and what goes wrong in production. Every article follows the same structure: a simple definition, the problem, the core concepts with diagrams and flows, practical guidance, common mistakes, and a quick check with answers. Key flows are interactive Archify diagrams you can zoom, explore through guided views and export.

The handbook has four modules and a self-review section:

  1. Kafka, Event-Driven Systems & Stream Processing: Kafka internals with KRaft, share groups and tiered storage; producers, consumers and exactly-once; event-driven architecture; schemas and event contracts; stream processing with Flink and Kafka Streams; and a real-time payments design.
  2. Distributed Transactions, CQRS, Saga & Consistency: consistency models, CAP, PACELC and consensus; why 2PC rarely fits microservices; outbox, CDC and idempotency; sagas; CQRS and event sourcing; and an order-processing design.
  3. Data Platforms, Governance & Data Security: lakehouse and streaming platforms, Iceberg and data mesh; governance, catalogs, lineage, quality and data contracts; data security and privacy engineering; and a regulated data platform design.
  4. Threat Modelling, AI Security & FinTech Regulations: threat modelling with STRIDE and friends; security architecture with zero trust, OWASP Top 10:2025, supply chain and post-quantum cryptography; AI and agent security; FinTech regulations (PCI DSS, DORA, PSD3, GDPR, the EU AI Act, and India’s DPDP, RBI and SEBI rules); and a secure payments platform design.
  5. Self Review & Quick Check: quick-check questions for every module, a design review checklist and a one-page quick reference.
