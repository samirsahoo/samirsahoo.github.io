Distributed Transactions, CQRS, Saga & Consistency

Keeping data correct across services: consistency models, CAP and PACELC, consensus, why two-phase commit rarely fits microservices, the outbox pattern, CDC and idempotency, sagas, CQRS and event sourcing.

Once data lives in more than one service, a single ACID transaction is no longer available. You have to choose a consistency model per use case. You also have to design for partial failure and make every step safe to retry.

Key conceptThe one idea to keep

Across service boundaries, replace “one big transaction” with local transactions + reliable events + idempotent consumers + compensations. Keep strong consistency inside a service, where an ACID database gives it to you cheaply.
