Purpose#

The Data, Distributed Systems & Cybersecurity Knowledge Hub explains event streaming, distributed data consistency, data platforms and security architecture simply, with diagrams and flows. It is written for architects, senior engineers, data engineers and security engineers, especially those working in financial services. It is a companion to the Java & Software Architecture Knowledge Hub, the Enterprise AI & Agentic AI Knowledge Hub and the Cloud Native & Platform Engineering Knowledge Hub.

Structure#

  1. Kafka, Event-Driven Systems & Stream Processing
  2. Distributed Transactions, CQRS, Saga & Consistency
  3. Data Platforms, Governance & Data Security
  4. Threat Modelling, AI Security & FinTech Regulations
  5. Self Review & Quick Check

Conventions#

Every article has a simple definition, the problem, core concepts with diagrams and flows, practical guidance, common mistakes, a quick check with answers, a summary and references. Key flows are interactive diagrams drawn with Archify. Smaller diagrams are Mermaid, kept in version control next to the text.

Versions and currency#

Content was reviewed on 1 November 2026. Versions it was checked against include:

  • Streaming: Apache Kafka 4.2 (KRaft only, share groups GA) and Apache Flink 2.2.
  • Data: Apache Iceberg format v3.
  • Security: OWASP Top 10:2025, the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2025 and the OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications 2026.
  • Regulation:
    • PCI DSS v4.0.1 and DORA, which has applied since January 2025.
    • The PSD3/PSR texts (agreed in 2025, applying from about 2027–28) and the EU AI Act as amended by the 2026 Digital Omnibus.
    • India’s DPDP Rules 2025 (phased to May 2027) and the RBI cyber security directions of July 2026.

This is not legal advice. Regulations, their dates and their interpretations change. Confirm obligations with your compliance and legal teams and with the primary texts.
