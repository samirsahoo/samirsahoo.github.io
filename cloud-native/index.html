Cloud native architecture handbook

Cloud Native & Platform Engineering Knowledge Hub

Understand the platform. See the flows. Check yourself.

Cloud native architecture explained simply: Kubernetes architecture, networking and security; containers, OpenShift, Helm, GitOps, CI/CD and release orchestration; platform engineering, internal developer platforms and developer experience; SRE, observability, resilience and FinOps — with interactive diagrams, system designs and quick checks.

Choose a learning pathSelf review & quick check

This handbook explains cloud native architecture the way an architect needs it: what each building block does, why it exists, how the pieces fit together, and what goes wrong in production. Every article follows the same structure: a simple definition, the problem, the core concepts with diagrams and flows, practical guidance, common mistakes, and a quick check with answers. Key flows are interactive Archify diagrams you can zoom, explore by guided view and export.

The handbook has four modules and a self-review section:

  1. Kubernetes Architecture, Networking & Security: control plane and nodes, workloads, scheduling and storage, networking from CNI to Gateway API, service mesh, and cluster security.
  2. Containers, OpenShift, Helm, GitOps & CI/CD: images and Docker, running Kubernetes in practice, OpenShift, Helm charts, CI/CD pipelines and progressive delivery, GitOps with Argo CD and Flux, and release orchestration with Digital.ai.
  3. Platform Engineering, IDPs & Developer Experience: platform as a product, internal developer platforms with Backstage and Crossplane, golden paths, developer experience, and a full platform design.
  4. SRE, Observability, Resilience & FinOps: SLOs and error budgets, incident management, OpenTelemetry, resilience and disaster recovery, cloud cost management, and a multi-region platform design.
  5. Self Review & Quick Check: quick-check questions for every module, a platform review checklist and a one-page quick reference.
